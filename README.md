## About the Project

Managing a modern real estate subdivision is a complex, multi-faceted endeavor. It requires maintaining flawless harmony between expanding land developments, prospective buyers, dedicated sales brokers, day-to-day collection ledgers, and the workforce on the ground.

**JLD Subdivision** is a purpose-built real estate enterprise platform designed to unify subdivision property administration, client contracts, broker management, collections, disbursements, and workforce payroll into one seamless, elegant workspace. Engineered from the ground up for subdivision developers, accounting teams, and property administrators, the platform turns fragmented spreadsheets and disconnected records into a single, synchronized source of truth.

Whether tracking the release of a newly opened phase, issuing an Official Receipt for an amortized lot, calculating broker commissions in real time, or preparing bi-monthly employee payroll, **JLD Subdivision** delivers clarity, speed, and uncompromising fiscal integrity at every step.

## Inside the Workspace

Like a well-orchestrated blueprint, the platform connects every operational domain of real estate development into an interdependent ecosystem:

### 🏡 Property & Inventory Management
Command full visibility over subdivision projects and phases—from Sto. Niño and Norala to Tupi and Surallah. Monitor total land area, planned block layouts, individual lot dimensions, cash pricing, and real-time inventory availability across every project stage.

### 📑 Buyer CRM & Lot Purchase Contracts
Guide buyers seamlessly from initial inquiry to contract signing. Structure tailored lot purchase agreements complete with installment terms, down payment schedules, interest amortization, and automated Statement of Account (SOA) ledgers that keep buyers informed and collections on track.

### 💰 Revenue & Collection Ledgers
Streamline payment remittance with a dual-layer accounting architecture. Issue serialized Official Receipts (OR) across various payment methods—cash, cheque, bank transfer, and e-wallets—while automatically allocating funds toward reservation fees, equity downpayments, or monthly installments.

### 🤝 Brokerage & Commission Tracking
Motivate and empower your sales network. With automated commission calculation tied directly to verified buyer collections, brokerage partners and property specialists enjoy transparent earnings records, clear commission claim histories, and up-to-the-minute balance statements.

### 👥 Workforce, Loans & Payroll Management
Keep your organization running smoothly with a dedicated human resources and payroll engine. Maintain employee records, track cash advances and salary adjustments, run automated cutoff payroll computations, and generate printable, itemized employee payslips with full deduction breakdowns.

### ⚖️ Cash Disbursements & Financial Integrity
Safeguard the company's financial health with certified expense vouchers, comprehensive cash inflow and outflow reporting, and an immutable compliance audit trail that records every transaction and administrative action.
